Concept

How to make AI usage a habit

A skill you don't use starts to fade. Use it in the same weekly moment and it won't.

Pick one or two moments that already happen on a schedule, like prepping for a standing meeting or doing your weekly plan. Bring AI into that moment on purpose, every time, and the habit builds itself.

  • A big one-time project doesn't build a habit. A small recurring one does.
  • Tie it to something that already happens weekly, so you don't have to remember on your own.
  • If you skip a week, just pick it back up next week. The habit doesn't need to be perfect.

A good gallery post covers three things: what you built, what tool or prompt you used, and what you'd do differently next time. Copy the prompt below and swap in your own details to get a draft.

Help me write a short gallery post about something I built with AI. Here's what I made: [describe what you built in one or two sentences]. Here's the tool or prompt I used: [paste your prompt or describe your approach]. Write it in three short parts: what it does, how I built it, and one thing I'd do differently next time. Keep the whole thing under 100 words and casual, not formal.
Why this prompt works

Naming the tool and the thing you'd fix is what makes a gallery post useful to the next person reading it, not just a highlight reel.

Read over what it gives you, then post it to the gallery yourself. You know your own project better than the model does, so fix anything that's off before it goes up.

🔧 Troubleshooting: I don't remember my exact prompt

Describe roughly what you asked for instead. "I asked it to reformat my resume and tighten the wording" is plenty.
